## 💡 Demo Tips

### Do's:
✅ **Speak clearly** when demonstrating voice input  
✅ **Pause for responses** - let the AI "wow" them  
✅ **Click on record links** - show the integration  
✅ **Try different languages** - showcase versatility  
✅ **Use real data** - authenticity matters  

### Don'ts:
❌ Don't rush through responses  
❌ Don't apologize for loading times (they're fast!)  
❌ Don't skip the voice demo - it's the showstopper  
❌ Don't ignore errors - handle gracefully if they occur  
❌ Don't over-promise - stick to implemented features

## 🎤 Voice Demo Best Practices

### Clear Speech Examples:

**Good Questions:**
- "What is today's sales?" (pause between words)
- "Show me top clients" (speak naturally)
- "Outstanding receivables" (clear pronunciation)

**Avoid:**
- Speaking too fast
- Mumbling or trailing off
- Background noise during recording
- Extremely long, complex questions

### Language-Specific Examples:

**Hindi:** "आज की बिक्री क्या है?"  
**Telugu:** "ఈరోజు అమ్మకాలు ఎంత?"  
**Tamil:** "இன்றைய விற்பனை என்ன?"  
**Malayalam:** "ഇന്നത്തെ വിൽപ്പന എന്താണ്?"
